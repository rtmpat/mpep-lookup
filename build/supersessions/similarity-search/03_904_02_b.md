## Summary

Revises the first paragraph of MPEP 904.02(b) to remind examiners that use of
Similarity Search is mandatory when examining a plant or utility application,
cross-referencing MPEP 719.05 and 902.03(e).

## Revised text

Detailed guidance on the choice and use of specific search tools can be established only within the context of the special requirements of each Technology Center (TC). However, a general methodology following a "decision tree" process, set forth below, for making broad decisions in search tool selection is suggested. Examiners are reminded that use of Similarity Search is mandatory when examining a plant or utility application. For more information on use of Similarity Search, see MPEP Section Section  719.05 and 902.03(e).
