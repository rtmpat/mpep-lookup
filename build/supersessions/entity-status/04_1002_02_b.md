## Summary

Adds item 51 to MPEP 1002.02(b): requests for reconsideration of a fine or
sanction based on a determination that an entity status claim was falsely made
and not in good faith (35 U.S.C. 41(j), 123(f) and 37 CFR 11.18(b)).

## Revised text

51. Requests for reconsideration of any assessed fine or issued sanction based upon a determination an entity status claim was falsely made and not in good faith under 35 U.S.C. 41(j), 123(f) and 37 CFR 11.18(b).
